Congratulations to Mathilde Bøkevoll, who defended her MSc thesis at the University of Bergen on 1 June 2026. Her [thesis](https://nva.sikt.no/registration/01a0d2f81cfe-b5014818-6727-4a0f-90c0-2ef20e277b41) analyses the wind flow downstream of a parabolic trough solar collector array.
