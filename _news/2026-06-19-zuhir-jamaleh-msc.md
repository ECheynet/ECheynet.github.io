Congratulations to Zuhir Jamaleh, who defended his MSc thesis at the University of Bergen on 19 June 2026. His [thesis](https://nva.sikt.no/registration/019f74c67ef5-b798bddf-1d5e-477a-87b1-cd5321afc7d0) investigates an alternative turbulence-coherence model for the design of a 22 MW offshore wind turbine.
