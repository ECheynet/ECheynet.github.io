Our [LOLLEX paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/amt-19-6267-2026) is out in _Atmospheric Measurement Techniques_. It documents how ship- and platform-mounted lidars were used to study flow within an operating offshore wind farm. The [associated lidar datasets]({% link _pages/software.md %}#open-datasets) are available on Zenodo.
