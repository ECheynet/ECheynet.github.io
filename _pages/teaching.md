## Curriculum development

Since 2022, I have led the redesign of ENERGI230, ENERGI321, and ENERGI322. The work introduced clearer thematic structures, aligned learning outcomes, project-based learning, assessment rubrics, quizzes, and guest lectures from industry. I also authored a 180-page set of lecture notes for ENERGI230.

I completed UiB's 200-hour university-pedagogy pathway (UPED 600–602), with additional training in inclusivity, assessment, artificial intelligence in teaching, and student supervision.
