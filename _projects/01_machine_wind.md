**Period:** 2024–2028  
**Programme:** Akademiavtale  
**Total funding:** approximately NOK 7 million

Machine Wind develops fast aerodynamic models for the optimal design and operation of offshore wind farms using supervised machine learning.

My role is **co-designer and co-PI**, contributing expertise in atmospheric turbulence and helping integrate realistic turbulence effects into the aerodynamic modelling framework.

[Project overview at the Bergen Offshore Wind Centre](https://www4.uib.no/en/research/research-centres/bergen-offshore-wind-centre-bow/featured/projects-at-bergen-offshore-wind-bow-centre)
