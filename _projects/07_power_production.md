- **Period:** 2020–2021
- **Programme:** UH-nett Vest
- **Funding:** approximately NOK 249,000

The project, _Estimering av langsiktig kraftproduksjon til flytende vindparker_, examined methods for estimating the long-term power production of floating wind farms. I co-designed the proposal and contributed expertise in offshore wind conditions and atmospheric data analysis.

The publication linked to the project in NVA examines wind and wave conditions at Utsira Nord and Sørlige Nordsjø II using the NORA3 hindcast dataset {% cite cheynet2024metocean %}.
